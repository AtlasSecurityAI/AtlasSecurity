The State of LLM Red Teaming 2024

Essential strategies for securing large language models against adversarial attacks and prompt injections in enterprise environments. As organizations race to adopt Generative AI, the attack surface expands significantly beyond traditional software vulnerabilities.

Evolving Threat Landscape

Traditional penetration testing methodologies fall short when dealing with the non-deterministic outputs of LLMs. Modern red teaming must now encompass prompt engineering, jailbreaking attempts, model extraction attacks, and training data poisoning.

Strategic Mitigation

Implementing guardrails at both the input and output layers is crucial. This includes semantic analysis of prompts to detect malicious intent and verification of generated content against corporate safety policies before it reaches the end-user.

GDPR in the Age of Agents

How autonomous AI agents challenge current data privacy regulations and what legal teams need to prepare for. The shift from passive tools to active agents introduces new complexities in data controlling and processing roles.

Autonomous Decision Making

Under GDPR Article 22, individuals have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing. AI agents that autonomously execute tasks—such as booking flights or managing finances—blur the lines of accountability and consent.

Data Minimization Challenges

Agents often require vast context windows to function effectively. Ensuring these agents adhere to data minimization principles while maintaining performance requires a new architectural approach to memory management and context retrieval.
